The three problems

parser: the shared date parser reads every date month first. All our tenants write the day first, so any day up to 12 swaps with the month: 7 April goes out as 4 July. Reports come in through the app, email and the form, from every tenant.

hint: the booking-v3 form tells people to type the month first. Its date field says MM/DD/YYYY, and then reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. People do what the hint says and get a swapped date. Only form reports from booking-v3 tenants. Fixing the parser does not fix this: the form needs the right hint.

one-day move: the date is off by one day, not swapped. “Invite says Tuesday 12 May, calendar says Monday 11 May”, “every booking in June is one day off”, “client in Lisbon got Tuesday, client in Toronto got Monday”, “the invite arrived before I made the booking”. These read like a time-zone or daylight-saving shift, not the parser. The desk noted them (desk-notes.json) but filed them under parser by tenant.

How they ended up in one ticket

Duplicates are merged on subject line alone (decision 0001). All three problems produce the same subjects (“Wrong day on the invite”, “Date is wrong”), so they merged into one ticket. Intake’s tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser and disagrees with the tenant’s cause on dozens of reports, so it can’t split them either.

For the desk

  • Count a ticket by cause, not by subject. causes.ts already knows two of the three.
  • A report that doesn’t read as a swapped date is a different problem. Give it a ticket, not a footnote.
